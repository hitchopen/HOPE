HOLLOW CAVITY RETAINED; SMOOTH REINFORCEMENT ADDED OUTWARD
The release master is 45deg_right_wrist_flange_mm.stl in this folder.
The archived thin-wall source CAD is not included in the release folder;
its provenance hash is retained below. No parametric STEP is supplied.

The full original hollow interior is retained for the gripper/hand
connector. There is NO solid core, new internal floor, or cavity-filling plug.

The hand side is the angled ring with four circumferential screw holes.
Its nominal circular wall at the opening is doubled outward: approximately
2.5 mm to 5.0 mm (nominal inner radius 17 mm retained; outer radius at the
opening increased from 19.5 mm to 22 mm). The original keyed inner profile
is retained, not replaced by a plain circular bore.

Reinforcement now extends over the outer body and external pocket fillets,
instead of stopping at a narrow band below the ring. The exterior expands
up to 2.5 mm radially away from the angled socket axis. Smooth quintic
transitions taper the addition to zero over 12 mm of height at the base,
and within 6 mm of the original screw recesses and rear tool-access
channels. This removes the abrupt ring-to-body step. The original base
mating face, locating pegs and mounting footprint are not expanded.

This is a nominal ring-wall dimension, not a uniform 5 mm normal thickness
over the entire body. The loft, taper zones and screw-access recesses have
varying wall thickness. At the opening, three radial checks measured
5.190, 5.001 and 4.983 mm, versus 2.690, 2.501 and 2.484 mm originally.
The keyed flat and screw-access recesses retain their required geometry.
In particular, the original screw-head seats remain at their original depth.
They are not shifted outward; no screw-length change is designed into this
revision.

Retained assembly features:
- Original 45-degree alignment and mating-plane locations.
- Full internal cavity, keyed hand socket, shoulder and engagement space.
- Four base screw passages, stepped counterbores and tool-access channels.
- Four inclined hand-side screw axes, bore diameters and head-seat depths.
- Two base locating pegs.

Construction started from the repaired hollow STL, mapped back to the
original CAD surfaces. Only the exterior loft and external pocket-fillet
surfaces were selected for remeshing and outward reinforcement. Exterior
remeshing used a 0.6 mm target edge length with a 0.015 mm surface-distance
limit to remove inherited sliver triangles before deformation. The outer
edge of the opening rim widens in its original tilted plane. No cavity
fill, global mesh smoothing, global scaling or rotation was applied.

PRINTING
Upload the STL rather than the STEP to Dremel Print Cloud. Select the
correct printer/material profile and import in millimetres at 100% scale.
Keep the modeled cavity and screw holes open. Do not run a fill-cavities,
make-solid or close-all-holes operation. Higher infill can be used within
the walls; 100% wall infill does not mean filling the modeled hollow cavity.
Place the model on the build plate and inspect the layer preview, especially
the angled socket, overhang supports, all screw holes and driver channels.
Remove supports from the socket and holes before assembly.

The additional outside material increases wrist mass and inertia. Check
clearance around the enlarged BODY as well as the ring against the hand
housing and neighboring hardware throughout the motion range. Check
connector, screw-head and driver fit before robot installation.
The original fastening geometry is retained, but printed fit is not verified.
Material, layer adhesion, print direction and process quality determine
impact performance; extra wall thickness alone does not establish a safe load
rating or guarantee improved impact strength. No FEA or physical impact
test has been performed. Test progressively in a controlled setup before
full-speed use.

The STL has been validated locally; upload/slicing in your Dremel account
and a physical print have not been tested. No printer-specific G-code is
included because the printer model and material were not specified.
