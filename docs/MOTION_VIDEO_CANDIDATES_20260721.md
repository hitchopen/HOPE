# Motion Video Candidates - 2026-07-21

Purpose: candidate real table-tennis human videos for retargeting into HOPE motion clips. The
current converted old HOPE motions are usable as a baseline, but final real hitting quality needs
more real-ball backhand, contact, footwork-arrive-hit-recover, and recovery segments.

Status: links only. No video content has been downloaded in this pass. Before using any public
video for training/retargeting, confirm permission/licensing and keep provenance with each clip.

## Screening Criteria

- Full body or near-full body visible through ready -> strike -> follow-through -> recover.
- Real ball contact preferred over shadow swing.
- Camera stable enough for pose estimation; side/front-oblique views are better than tight crops.
- Backhand coverage is highest priority because the old HOPE motion set has only two backhand files.
- Segmentable single strokes are better than long rally footage unless the rally has clean repeated
  cycles.
